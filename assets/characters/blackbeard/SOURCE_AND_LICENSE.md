# Blackbeard — source and rights

## Authored anatomy
- Blender Studio **Human Base Meshes v1.4.1**, realistic male anatomy by **Dan Ulrich**.
- Official archive: https://download.blender.org/demo/asset-bundles/human-base-meshes/human-base-meshes-bundle-v1.4.1.zip
- SHA-256: `811f43accbb31a88266d932f8f5563b2d13586fca0ba2693aad1f5fe582b3515`
- Bundle README explicitly licenses provided assets **CC0**. Only `GEO-body_male_realistic` and its two eyes were imported. No third-party rig or executable script was imported/executed; Blender auto-execution was disabled. The separate Rain Rig license text in the bundle does not describe the selected anatomy.
- Modified proportions, facial expression, eye treatment, muscle definition, skin colors and hidden-surface removal for this character.

## New work
Hair and beard meshes, clothing, harness, chains, boots, armor, accessories, firearm, optional tricorn, materials/textures, skeleton and animation clips were authored in this task. Fabric/leather/wood/hair texture patterns are original procedural artwork, not downloaded texture packs. No game-extracted assets or commercial character meshes were used.

Editable native meshes and a consolidated skinned GLB, with Idle, Walk, Run, firearm Attack and optional Melee motions. These are authored game-cycle animations, not mocap, facial animation, cloth simulation or a guarantee of art-direction acceptance. Actual runtime QA results and known limitations are recorded separately.
